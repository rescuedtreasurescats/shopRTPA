Before you browse

A few good
questions.

Can I order yet?

Not quite! We’re getting the first collection ready. The previews show our ideas, not products currently available to buy.

How will apparel ordering work?

We plan to link apparel to Amazon, where you’ll choose your options and complete your order. Once products are ready, each listing will show you exactly where to buy.

Will there be cat and dog designs?

Absolutely. This is one shop for supporters of both cats and dogs, with rescue-logo apparel and more designs planned.

How do purchases help?

Our aim is to earn proceeds from merchandise sales to support the rescue. Product details will explain the arrangement when ordering opens.
