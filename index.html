Good to know

A few good
questions.

Where do I place my order?

Start with the shopping link for the item or fundraiser you want. You’ll choose your options, pay, and receive order confirmation on the selling partner’s website. Shop RTPA does not take payments.

How does my purchase help RTPA?

Rescued Treasures earns a royalty on its Amazon Merch apparel. Our other fundraisers will have their own earnings arrangements, which we’ll explain when they open. The amount can vary by product and partner. Purchases are merchandise orders, not direct donations.

Do I need a special link or code?

Use the shopping links on this site. If a partner requires a fundraiser code, we’ll show it beside the link. Shopping directly with a vendor without RTPA’s link or code may not credit our rescue.

Can I buy from different partners in one order?

Each partner has its own checkout. Items bought from different partners are separate orders, with their own shipping charges, delivery estimates, and policies.

Where can I find options, prices, and shipping costs?

The selling partner’s product page and checkout show current options, prices, availability, and delivery details. For apparel, check Amazon for sizes, colors, and the size chart. Our illustrations are previews; actual garment details and print placement may vary.

Who helps with tracking, returns, or an order problem?

Contact the partner you ordered from using your confirmation email or its order-support page. Each partner sets its own shipping and return policies. For Amazon apparel, open “Your Orders” in your Amazon account.

What can I shop for right now?

Our Rescued Treasures apparel is available on Amazon. Both coffee applications have been submitted and are awaiting approval; coffee fundraising links aren’t live yet. Sweet treats are planned, with no ordering open. We’ll update this page as each fundraiser becomes available.

How can I reach the rescue?

For questions about RTPA or this shop, email rescuedtreasurescats@gmail.com. For payment, delivery, or return questions about a purchase, contact the selling partner.
