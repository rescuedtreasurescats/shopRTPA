Good to know

A few good
questions.

Where do I place my order?

Use any “Shop on Amazon” link to open our collection. Select a product, choose your options, and complete your purchase on Amazon. This website does not take payments.

How does my purchase help?

Rescued Treasures earns a royalty on sales of its Amazon Merch apparel. The amount varies by product and selling price. Your purchase is a merchandise order, not a direct donation.

Which sizes, colors, and prices are available?

The Amazon listing has the current options, size chart, price, and delivery estimate. Our illustrations show the design; actual garment details and print placement may vary.

What about delivery, returns, or an order problem?

Amazon handles apparel orders and customer service. Open “Your Orders” in your Amazon account for tracking, returns, and help with a purchase.

Can I order the sweet treats yet?

Not yet. They’re part of our plans, but we haven’t opened ordering. We’ll add vendor details and purchase information here when they’re ready.

How can I reach the rescue?

For a question about the rescue or this shop, email rescuedtreasurescats@gmail.com. For help with an Amazon order, use Amazon’s order support.
